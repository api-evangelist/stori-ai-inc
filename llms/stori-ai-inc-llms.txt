# STORI AI Inc.

> STORI AI is a San Francisco-based AI content-marketing platform that helps brands define their
> identity — archetype, tone, tagline, and colors — and turn that identity into on-brand social posts,
> graphics, images, videos, and go-to-market marketing materials. The product generates personalized
> branded content, schedules and publishes it across digital platforms, and offers analytics, content
> storage, and multi-brand, multi-language, team-collaboration tiers. STORI AI is a web SaaS
> application for marketers and brand teams.
>
> NOTE FOR AGENTS: as of 2026-08-13 STORI AI publishes NO public developer API, no OpenAPI or other
> machine-readable contract, no SDKs on any public package registry, no MCP server, and no A2A agent
> card. There is no programmatic surface to call. This file is a catalog profile generated by API
> Evangelist, not a provider-published document.
